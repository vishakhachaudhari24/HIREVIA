Job Title: Data Analyst (Fresher / Entry-Level)

About the Role:
We are looking for a motivated and detail-oriented Data Analyst (Fresher) 
to join our growing analytics team based in London, UK. 
This role is ideal for recent graduates or candidates with up to 1 year of experience 
who are eager to build a career in data analytics.

Required Skills:
- Python (basic knowledge of Pandas, NumPy)
- SQL (basic queries, joins)
- Excel (formulas, pivot tables)
- Data visualization (Power BI, Tableau, or similar)
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Basic understanding of statistics

Nice to Have:
- Internship or project experience in data analytics
- Knowledge of Machine Learning basics
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau dashboards
- Familiarity with ETL concepts
- Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)

Minimum Experience:
0–1 year of experience (internships and academic projects included)

Location:
Remote (UK residents only)

Work Authorization:
Must be authorised to work in the UK. No sponsorship available.

Responsibilities:
- Collect, clean, and organise datasets from multiple sources
- Perform basic data analysis and generate insights
- Create dashboards and reports for business teams
- Assist in identifying trends and patterns in data
- Work with team members to understand business requirements
- Present findings in a simple and clear format

Salary:
£25,000 - £35,000 per annum
